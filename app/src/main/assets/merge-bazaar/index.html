🎪 Merge Bazaar

How to Play: Tap a Generator Crate (📦 / 🧺 / 🎁) to spawn a Level-1 item. Drag and drop an item onto another identical item to merge it, or drag it to an empty tile to move it. You can also tap two identical items to merge. To serve a customer: tap an Order at the top, then tap the matching item on the board to earn 🪙 Coins & ⭐ 1 Star! Unlock stalls at 3 ⭐ and 6 ⭐. 3 stars = 1 Level up (+20 ⚡).
